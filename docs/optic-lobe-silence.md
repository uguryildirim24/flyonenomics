# Why the optic lobe is silent

**Cause: inhibitory visual relays without a depolarizing operating point.**
The declared sensory-only substrate drives the photoreceptors but not L1/L2.
Photoreceptor inhibition reaches the lamina and the stripe changes lamina
voltage, but releasing inhibition does not bring those cells to spike
threshold. Their spike output is zero, not merely an unchanged nonzero rate.
This is a property of the specified model, **not a missing connection, a
zeroed weight row, an index mix-up, or a discovered implementation defect**.

Adding background to the optic lobe makes lamina cells excitable at the same
thresholds. It does not, by itself, establish working vision through to TuBu.
Restoring the old photoreceptor signs can also make the lamina fire, but
reverses its stripe response and leaves the inhibitory ON relay silent.
Neither counterfactual is a new calibration.

## Question and scope

This diagnoses the lamina break in t-0010's corrected WP18 run (`5845f72`):
a 5° dark stripe changes R1–6 firing, but produces exactly zero
stripe-minus-ambient firing change from L1/L2 onward at 50–300 Hz light input.
The diagnostic does not select a new resting substrate or a visual calibration.

Reproducer: `scripts/diagnose_optic_lobe.py`. Compact evidence:
`validation/records/p2/optic-lobe-silence.json`, generated by
`scripts/summarize_optic_lobe.py`. Raw counts, exact engine-index/root-ID
inventories, weight audits and logs are in `camber-runs/diag/optic-lobe/`.

## What the specified model actually drives

`data/drive-v0.2.yaml` assigns 1 mV background events to **sensory** cells only.
The optic group (77,529 cells), visual-projection group (8,053), and all other
groups receive zero direct background. `drive/rest.py:rest_background_weights`
expands those explicit group weights; `drive/background.py:group_indices`
uses annotation super-class, with DAN/KC/ER overrides. “Sensory” does not mean
“every cell that processes sensory information.”

The loaded populations agree with that rule:

## Ambient activity and release of inhibition

On the declared substrate, **every recorded L1/L2, medulla, T5, LC/LPLC,
MeTu and TuBu population emitted zero spikes in all 12 conditions** (three
seeds × ambient/two stripes/dark). Thus zero contrast is not cancellation of
left/right responses, nor a live baseline that simply ignores the stripe.
R1–6 averaged 91.347 Hz in ambient light and 0.382 Hz in darkness; its
ipsilateral stripe changes were −14.648 Hz left and −14.551 Hz right.
Even near-removal of photoreceptor firing in darkness did not make L1/L2 fire.

The signal **does reach lamina voltage**. Three-seed mean sampled voltage in
the ipsilateral columns, mV:

| Column | Ambient | Stripe | Stripe minus ambient | Dark |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| L1 left | −57.957 | −57.358 | +0.599 | −52.025 |
| L1 right | −60.629 | −59.821 | +0.808 | −52.041 |
| L2 left | −57.704 | −57.065 | +0.639 | −52.024 |
| L2 right | −60.574 | −59.775 | +0.800 | −52.040 |

The stripe releases inhibition in the expected direction, but the voltage
stays subthreshold. Whole-population ambient means are −57.038 mV (L1) and
−57.015 mV (L2). The largest sampled L1/L2 voltage anywhere across these
conditions is −51.827 mV, still 6.827 mV below threshold.

## Counterfactual measurements

Three-seed means, Hz per neuron. Ambient uses the whole population; stripe
changes use the **same ipsilateral column** in paired ambient and stripe
runs. L1 columns contain 68 left / 96 right cells; L2 contains 65 / 98.
Whole-side, contralateral and per-seed results remain in the evidence record.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Declared | L1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Declared | L2 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Optic +1 mV | L1 | 0.108 | −0.010 | 0 | 0.476 |
| Optic +1 mV | L2 | 0.057 | −0.003 | 0 | 0.254 |
| Optic +2 mV | L1 | 12.708 | +1.284 | +1.217 | 27.979 |

| Connection-file signs | L1 | 5.931 | −1.527 | −1.321 | 0 |

At +1 mV, ambient lamina firing appears but the narrow-stripe response is not
a consistent positive contrast. At +2 mV, **both L1 and L2 increase on both
sides in every seed**, without changing their −45 mV thresholds. L1's six
paired changes range from +0.956 to +1.721 Hz; L2's from +0.066 to +0.331 Hz.
This is causal support for a missing depolarizing operating point, not a
claim that 2 mV is an appropriate biological background or a new substrate.
Both amplitudes lie within the existing model's 0–2 mV background range.
Because the intervention drives the whole optic group, it does not isolate
the minimal cell types or strength that would need tonic excitation.

The downstream measurements also limit that result:

| Arm | Population | Ambient Hz | Left stripe ΔHz | Right stripe ΔHz |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Optic +2 mV | Mi1 | 9.217 | −0.433 | −0.433 |

Mi1 and Tm3 decrease on every +2 mV stripe/seed/side, consistent with increased
inhibitory L1 output. **LPLC, MeTu and TuBu remain at zero spikes in every
condition of every arm.** LC10 has small background activity under optic
drive (0.013 Hz ambient at +2 mV), but no change in the probed columns.
Simply extending background has not restored the full visual pathway.

The connection-sign control activates L1/L2 but gives the *opposite* stripe
response: less photoreceptor excitation means less lamina firing. Mi1/Tm3
stay silent despite this activation, recapitulating the next inhibitory
bottleneck. A positive connection-file sign is therefore not a missing
software correction to apply to the histaminergic model.

## Signs and connectivity

The photoreceptor rows are present, nonzero, and inhibitory **as specified**.
`substrate/transmitters.py:classify_rows` assigns photoreceptors His;
`substrate/scales.py:scale_array` multiplies each original signed row by
`(curated_sign / connection_file_sign) × transmitter_gain`.
`drive/rest.py:apply_rest_substrate` checks the canonical scale SHA-256 and
`engine/brian_engine.py:set_weight_scale` applies it to the base weights.
Thus a positive connection-file row becomes negative, rather than being
silently deleted. His gain is 1; Glu gain is 4; optic exemption is false.

| Projection | Connection pairs | Anatomical synapses | Nonzero negative pairs after composition | Effective signed weight sum (mV) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| R1–6 → L1 | 5,867 | 63,344 | 5,867 | −17,419.600 |
| R1–6 → L2 | 5,727 | 62,051 | 5,727 | −17,064.025 |
| L1 → Mi1 | 1,743 | 121,693 | 1,743 | −133,862.300 |
| L1 → Tm3 | 8,829 | 80,600 | 8,829 | −88,660.000 |

R1–6 reaches 1,247 of 1,579 L1 cells and 1,248 of 1,554 L2 cells in this subset.
Coverage is incomplete, but missing connectivity cannot explain silence in
*every* connected lamina cell. R1–6 pair weights range from −17.05 to −0.275 mV
onto L1 and from −20.90 to −0.275 mV onto L2. No audited pair is zero.
L2 → Tm1 and L2 → Tm2 are entirely excitatory after composition (1,876 and
2,526 pairs). The first silent relay can therefore also deprive downstream
excitatory paths of their input.

The diagnostic “connection-sign” arm does exactly WP20 4.8r's histamine-off
recomposition: set the scale of **all photoreceptor-origin rows** to 1,
leaving every other scale unchanged. This is not deletion of histamine
synapses. It restores mixed connection-file signs, predominantly excitatory:
4,753/5,867 R1–6 → L1 pairs and 4,622/5,727 R1–6 → L2 pairs become positive.
It is a mechanistic control, not a biologically justified repair.

## Threshold mechanism

The declared substrate has no threshold spread (`threshold: null`,
`sigma_th = 0`). Its base threshold is −45 mV; rest and reset are −52 mV.
The `lif` engine implements

```
dv/dt = (v_0 - v + g) / 20 ms
dg/dt = -g / 5 ms
spike when v > v_th_i
synaptic event: g += weight × postsynaptic gain
```

There is no intrinsic tonic current or post-inhibitory rebound mechanism.
For a silent cell starting at rest and receiving only negative current,
removing that current brings voltage back toward −52 mV, not across −45 mV.
More photoreceptor input makes that inhibition stronger. Less input cannot
create missing excitation. The measured thresholds are the specified −45 mV,
not an accidental infinite threshold or silencing flag.

For scale, 100 background inputs at 10 Hz each give mean isolated-cell
`g = 100 × 10/s × 0.005 s × w_bg = 5 × w_bg` mV. A 1 mV event weight therefore
supplies a 5 mV mean drive, below the 7 mV rest-to-threshold gap even *before*
photoreceptor inhibition; fluctuations and recurrence still matter. A 2 mV
weight supplies 10 mV. These are model arithmetic, not physiological estimates
or target firing rates. The counterfactuals use these two weights to test
excitability, not to fit a desired output.

## What would have to change for vision

Item 122's resting substrate remains the declared substrate. A calm central
brain and a working sugar reflex do not establish a usable operating point
for every inhibitory sensory relay. This diagnostic does not rescore the
rest search or silently extend the canonical background configuration.

An eye-to-brain visual model needs an independently justified depolarizing
operating point for the lamina and other inhibitory relays, or a cell/synapse
model that represents the relevant graded and sign-inverting transmission.
The Glu ×4 L1 → Mi1/Tm3 stage and undriven visual-projection populations also
need examination; fixing only the first gate is not enough. The experiments
show model-sized excitation can cross the existing thresholds, not that
these background weights are measured physiological inputs.

A proposed change would have to be declared explicitly and then remeasured
for ambient brain activity, retinal-to-lamina transmission, deeper ON/OFF
relays, central input, and steering, with sided and shuffled-encoder controls.
Do not flip histamine to excitation just to obtain spikes, lower thresholds
to fit a desired firing number, or call nonzero background activity a visual
signal. The current measurements identify a missing model mechanism, not a
new pass line or a finished repair.

## Relation to Phase 1 and the TuBu path

WP10 (`docs/visual-path-diagnostic.md`) already found a failure of propagation,
but its precise break was different: the Phase 1 connection-file signs let
R1–6 excite L1/L2, then the inhibitory L1 → Mi1/Tm3 relay had no baseline to
modulate. Phase 2 deliberately corrects photoreceptor sign to histaminergic
inhibition, so the same missing operating-point problem can appear one
stage earlier. WP10 is not evidence that L1/L2 were already silent under the
same signs as today's substrate.

The separate TuBu experiment injects synthetic visual input downstream of
the optic lobe. This diagnostic does not invalidate that experiment: it can
measure the response of central circuits and steering to that injected
signal. It cannot establish retinal-to-brain transmission, repair the silent
eye pathway, or demonstrate natural visual attention. Any successful TuBu
behaviour must remain labelled as downstream visual-input substitution.
