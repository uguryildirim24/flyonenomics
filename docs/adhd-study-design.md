# MaleCNS dopamine-clearance/history study — pre-run design

Status: Part A only. No stage 5a or 5b run is authorised until the coordinator's go. This first commit precedes the cost probe and all new neural outcomes. The following design is transcribed verbatim from `tasks/t-0077.md`; implementation details follow it.

## Design from the brief (verbatim)

**Question.** In the MaleCNS male brain model, does removing dopamine uptake (the fumin mutant's broken transporter) change a history-dependent representation of two competing inputs in the ring (`ER`) neurons, and does reducing dopamine release move it back?

**Substrate.** The male resting substrate minted by lane t-0076 (port step 6, branch `hp/flyonenomics/t-0076-set-up-the-male-brain-as-our-starting-br`; not merged yet). `CX_DAN` is not driven in any arm (its resting activity only). Input is injected at TuBu; the fly does not see (items 127 to 133).

**Conditions (2 × 2).** Wild type or fumin, crossed with vehicle or release reduction.
- fumin = named `fumin` (`ScaleDat` all compartments, factor 0). `k_ns` stays.
- Release reduction = `ScaleRelease(factor=f)` applied globally, named a *synthesis-inhibitor-like release reduction*, never a drug dose. **f is fixed from chemistry alone, before any neural outcome:** the fraction at which fumin's resting steady-state pool equals wild type's `DA_ref`, i.e. `f = k_ns·DA_ref / (Vmax·DA_ref/(Km+DA_ref) + k_ns·DA_ref)`. With today's male constants (`data/dopamine-male-cns-v1.0.yaml`: DA_ref 0.02, Vmax 0.11, Km 1.3, k_ns 0.05) that is 0.375; recompute it from the adopted files and code and freeze the exact value. Same f in both genotypes.
- **Initial pools:** each condition starts its pools at that condition's analytic resting steady state (release at wild-type resting DAN rates divided by that condition's clearance), computed from the chemistry code, not from a neural run; state the numbers. Report measured pool drift during settle as an audit. Rationale: fumin's pool time constant is about 1/k_ns = 20 s, so settling from `DA_ref` would take minutes of brain time per arm.

**Stage 5a (prerequisite, wild type vehicle only).** Male `ER` position code: off plus four TuBu positions, 10 fresh seeds (indices 101 to 110), 20 s each (after settle). Decode with whole seeds held out, as in item 151. Then choose the pair by item 146 (iv)'s rule, written into the design doc **before** 5a runs: both members individually give reliable, distinguishable `ER` patterns at comparable drive, opposite hemifields if the representation supports it, not the pair with the largest decoding distance. The A-versus-B template axis comes from 5a data only. If no position is reliably represented, stop with `ha waiting`: that is a prerequisite failure, reported as such.

**Stage 5b (the experiment).** Item 146 (iii)'s eight protocols (blank prefix → off, A, B, A+B; A prefix → off, A+B; B prefix → off, A+B), same fixed gap, no state reset, matched external event realisations across all arms of a seed, × the four conditions = 32 arms, 10 fresh seeds (indices 201 to 210). Declare the prefix, gap and test durations and the response window before the run (keep item 146's timing unless the cost probe forces shorter; say which).

**Readouts.** Per seed and condition, `H = [μ(A prefix, A+B) − μ(A prefix, off)] − [μ(B prefix, A+B) − μ(B prefix, off)]` projected onto the 5a A-versus-B axis (item 146 (v)).
- **Primary 1 (genotype):** `ΔH_gen = H(fumin, vehicle) − H(wild type, vehicle)`.
- **Primary 2 (rescue):** `ΔΔH = [H(fumin, release) − H(wt, release)] − [H(fumin, vehicle) − H(wt, vehicle)]`. Movement back toward wild type means ΔΔH has the opposite sign to ΔH_gen.
- Both: paired across seeds, 95% whole-seed bootstrap intervals (seed is the unit), Holm over the two. No equivalence claims and no invented bound: an interval that includes zero is written "no detected difference".
- **Key secondary (interpretation):** the per-cell affine control from item 146 (v) and the research doc §4: predict fumin's pair responses from wild type by a per-cell nonnegative scale plus offset fitted on single-stimulus/off controls only, and report the residual of ΔH_gen beyond it.
- **Recorded, descriptive:** EB and every compartment's pool trajectory, receptor occupancies, DAN, TuBu, `ER` and central rates, time-resolved `ER` responses, per-seed H. Input audit: the encoder caps summed stripe drive (`behaviour/encoder.py:110-128`); report actual TuBu spikes for A, B and A+B.

**Claim ceiling.** A candidate neural signature relevant to stimulus selection under an ADHD-relevant dopamine perturbation in a fixed model; not ADHD, not attention, not behaviour, not a drug dose (research doc §4).

### Pair rule (must not maximise distance)

The decoder is nearest training-seed centroid, Euclidean distance after cellwise standard deviation scaling learned inside each leave-one-whole-seed-out fold (scale floor 1 Hz). Publish the four-way position confusion matrix and five-way matrix including off, not just accuracy.

**Clarification (25 September 2026, after outcomes; original freeze above unchanged):** The executed `scripts/adhd_analysis.py` already committed before outcomes uses the *mean squared* cellwise-standardized distance, `D_k = mean_i[((x_i - centroid_ki)/scale_i)^2]`, and each held-out margin is `D_wrong - D_correct`. The frozen prose calls it Euclidean distance; squaring preserves nearest-centroid labels, but not margin magnitudes or their bootstrap intervals. This documents the executed rule; it does not recompute the pair or retrospectively amend the selection rule.

For each candidate pair, evaluate binary held-out discrimination for A vs off, B vs off, and A vs B. For **each member separately**, define a held-out margin as distance to the wrong centroid minus distance to the correct centroid, averaged across cells. Bootstrap the ten seed margins 10,000 times; require the lower two-sided 95% interval above zero for both classes in each binary comparison. This is a statistical prerequisite for representation, not a biological rate floor or effect-size bound. Publish every candidate's margins; this pilot's pair screening is not a new confirmatory hypothesis family.

Examine pairs in fixed anatomical-label order: `(−50,+50)`, `(−150,+150)`, `(−150,+50)`, `(−50,+150)`, then `(−150,−50)`, `(+50,+150)`. Choose the **first** with individually represented members and discriminability; never rank by distance. The first four are opposite hemifields. Also require linearly independent baseline-subtracted templates (report Gram matrix/condition number); a rank-deficient pair cannot support the two-template interpretation. If none qualifies, stop with `ha waiting`, prerequisite failure, rather than trying another stimulus strength. Freeze the selected pair and all 5a raw hashes in a commit before 5b.

Let `tA=mean(A−off)`, `tB=mean(B−off)` in Hz, `d=tA−tB`. Freeze `axis=d/(d·d)`, so the single-input A-minus-B difference projects to one unit. No cell selection or axis refitting on 5b. Fit unrestricted A/B mixture coefficients by least squares on these two baseline-subtracted templates; **no sum-to-one constraint**, retain residual norm/vector and condition number. Neither coefficient alone establishes suppression.

### Frozen inference

- Rates in the primary 2 s window enter H. Compute per-seed cellwise H before projection and the two specified paired contrasts. 10,000 seed-block percentile bootstrap resamples, RNG seed 14620260922. Every resample includes all conditions/protocols of each selected seed. Independently resample the ten 5a seeds and refit the axis with the pair held fixed; this carries template uncertainty, not pair-selection uncertainty.
- Two-sided **exact paired sign-randomisation** p values enumerate all 1,024 sign assignments for each contrast, conditional on the frozen axis, then Holm across exactly the two primaries. Report unadjusted 95% bootstrap intervals and Holm-adjusted p values separately: an interval containing zero means **no detected difference**, and a contrast not surviving Holm is not a multiplicity-controlled finding. Seeds are stochastic replicates of one anatomical model, not flies.
- For the affine secondary, training controls are the 5b blank-prefix off/A/B arms only. Fit per-cell nonnegative scale plus unrestricted offset, leaving out the entire evaluated seed. Correct covariance and predictor variance of the three training stimulus means for finite-seed sampling noise (including paired noise covariance); scale is `max(0, corrected_cov/corrected_var)`. If predictor variance is nonpositive, use scale zero and mean offset and report that cell as unresolved, not as evidence for an affine mechanism. Apply to held-out wild-type pair/off history vectors. The offset cancels in H but remains in response predictions. Bootstrap whole seeds and refit all folds; duplicate copies of a held seed are excluded from its training fold. Report the residual `H_fumin − H_affine(wt)` alongside raw ΔH_gen; do not replace either primary.
- Describe J, mixture coefficients/residuals, H by seed and 100 ms time bins, unsubtracted responses, receptor terms, all 37 pools, settle pool drift, actual DAN/CX_DAN/TuBu/ER/central firing. Audit signed composed ER→ER edges and presynaptic events. This is not an inhibition ablation or proof of mutual inhibition.
- A opposite-signed difference-in-differences describes the prespecified direction toward wild type. Also report both genotype gaps and whether their absolute magnitudes shrink or overshoot; opposite sign alone does not prove a therapeutic rescue.
