# Frozen confirmatory follow-up — genotype contrast only (pre-outcome)

**Status: proposed freeze; no confirmatory neural outcome may be run until the freeze commit, short diagnostic, pinned box gate and Rolf's go.** The design is independent of seeds 301–340. The 10-seed pilot (`docs/adhd-study-results.md`) had ΔH_gen −0.085156 [−0.153954, −0.020968], exact sign p 36/1024, Holm p 0.0703125. A 40-seed follow-up is planned against a smaller possible effect, not chosen from follow-up data; the interaction is not tested.

## One question, one decision

For seed *s*, calculate per-cell `H = rate(A prefix, AB test) − rate(A prefix, off test) − rate(B prefix, AB test) + rate(B prefix, off test)` from the first [0,2) s of the test; project onto the **unchanged** 5a-01 A-minus-B template axis `d/(d·d)` fitted on 10 calibration seeds, with pair (−50°, +50°) fixed. Primary ΔH_gen is H(fumin, vehicle) minus H(wild type, vehicle), paired by seed. Independent template units are dimensionless projections of Hz. **Confirm** iff the two-sided paired sign-randomisation p < 0.05 **and** the sample estimate is negative. Otherwise **not confirmed**. Report the estimate and nominal 95% interval either way. An interval containing zero is written **“no detected difference.”** Exactly one primary; no Holm. Statistical significance does not identify a mechanism.
