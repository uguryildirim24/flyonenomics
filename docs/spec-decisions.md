# Spec decisions

Dispositions of every review finding, appended per round by fable. "Accepted" names the change made in `docs/SPEC.md`; "rejected" gives the reason. A rejected finding may be re-argued once by gpt with new evidence; if still rejected it goes to "Open for Rolf".

## Round 3 (responding to `docs/spec-review-1.md`)

1. Rescue gate citation mismatch (blocker). Accepted. Andretic 2005 used 3-iodotyrosine and methamphetamine on an electrophysiological endpoint, not methylphenidate on walking fixation. Tests 4.4 and 4.5 are now "prediction, recorded", not gated; level 4 passes on 4.0 to 4.3 only. Cirl is stated as not represented and nothing is claimed equivalent to it (sections 1.2, 7, 10 item 3).

2. Calibration and validation conflated (blocker). Accepted. Section 7 now tags every test as verification, consistency, calibration, empirical, prediction, or sensitivity. Calibration is three bounded procedures (K1 background, K2 release constants, K3 behaviour) run once by `scripts/calibrate.py` and frozen into versioned data files with records. The Brembs flies are split into a calibration half and a holdout half (`data/brembs-split.json`); K3 uses only the calibration half and test 4.3 only the holdout. Every status entry carries a binding block (commit, `uv.lock` hash, provenance hash, data versions, assay, machine) and manifests report stale bindings.

3. Work-package cycles (blocker). Accepted. DA baseline measurement moved from WP3 to WP5 (K2). Test 0.6 (orchestrator replay) moved from WP1 to WP4; WP1 keeps the engine-level half of 0.7. WP6's full acceptance with layers on is integration gate I3 in WP9; WP6 itself accepts with layers off. WP7 and WP8 depend on WP4 only, using fixtures. Owners added for shared types (`types.py`, WP0), the validation runner skeleton (WP0), calibration script (WP0 skeleton, sections filled by WP3, WP5, WP6), `docs/bench.md` and `docs/calibration.md`. Four integration gates I1 to I4 listed with owners. The graph is acyclic.

4. Annotation contract unverified (blocker). Accepted. Section 3.2 now treats the v783 release, filename, and columns as expected values that WP0 verifies and records; `registry/annotations.py` is a schema adapter that fails at import with missing names. Selectors, expected count ranges, `required` flags, `exclude` lists, and declared overlaps are YAML fields; registry construction fails on a missing or out-of-range required population. Explicit root lists per connectome version are produced by a supervoxel-ID bridge between the v630 and v783 annotation files (`map_roots`), with unmapped counts recorded.

5. Compartment representation (blocker). Accepted. Replaced the single integer compartment with two sparse weighted matrices: innervation M (compartment by neuron, columns sum to 1, equal split across a DAN's compartments as a placeholder) and exposure W (neuron by compartment, rows sum to 1 or 0). Effective concentration is `W @ DA`; release into a compartment is `alpha_c * (M @ s)_c`. Precedence of receptor entries over overlapping populations is declared and checked. SciPy is now a runtime dependency (section 2.1).

6. Dopamine calibration and elapsed-time semantics (blocker). Accepted. K2 sets a per-compartment release constant so that every innervated compartment's wild-type tonic level equals a single reference `DA_ref`; under-innervated compartments are flagged and capped. Settle has a convergence criterion (windowed relative change in DA and rates, minimum 2 s, maximum 10 s), and unconverged settles flag the run. The slow-state approximation is named (`phase1-drug-only`), described (neural state and pools discarded at every probe start; only drug concentrations carry), recorded in every manifest, and its consequence for `repeat` is printed in summaries.

7. Neuromodulation overwrites interventions (blocker). Accepted. Section 3.4 now has `neuromod/state.py` with immutable base arrays, persistent genotype and drug layers, a dopamine layer, and a fixed composition order ending with the silenced mask forcing the unreachable threshold after clipping. Test 0.7 verifies persistence across 200 chunks with modulation active.

8. Receptor-null semantics (major). Accepted. The named genotype is renamed `dop1r1_evoked_null`, the deviation formulation's consequences are stated in section 3.4, test 3.7 is labelled a verification of the equations, and the alternative absolute formulation is item 10 for Rolf.

9. Drug units and combination (major). Accepted. 3-iodotyrosine now goes through the same pharmacokinetics as methylphenidate and its EC50 is in brain µM. Every drug parameter names its compartment and unit. Composition rules stated: release factors multiply (`rel0 * rel_geno * rel_drug`), DAT factors multiply on Vmax, methylphenidate acts only on Km. Section 5 gained a range column for placeholders and assumptions; sensitivity suites 3.9 and 4.8 sweep them.

10. DAN fast synapses versus pools (major). Accepted. Section 3.4 states that the fast signed DAN connections are retained by default, adds the `dan_fast_synapses` layer flag with a `disconnect` alternative, and test 3.8 runs the three-way ablation; no result is called dopamine-specific without it.

11. Engine interface gaps (major). Accepted. Section 3.1 now has `set_input_population` (topology fixed for the probe; union of all drive targets; summed rates for overlaps), `set_input_rates`, `disconnect`, `set_weight_scale` (per-connection, file order), `record_traces`, `seed`, `tick`, and full-length array contracts with dtypes and units. Background weights are a full-length array.

12. Reproducibility state contract (major). Accepted. Section 3.6 defines seed derivation via `SeedSequence(S, s)` with four named child streams and per-probe engine seeds; the probe sequence (restore, reseed, reset, apply, settle, clear) is fixed; spikes are stored as integer ticks; the index-to-root table is stored per seed; the reference artefact is pinned in `data/reference/`; test 0.3 distinguishes exact replay within the pinned environment from statistical replay elsewhere.

13. Numerical acceptance expectations (major). Accepted. Test 0.2 uses a deterministic `SpikeGeneratorGroup` fixture with an explicit event-matching rule; test 0.1 uses a fixed input; test 1.1 is scoped to background off; test 1.4 is the background-on version as a difference over seeds.

14. Artefact metric reference values (major). Accepted. The synchrony statistic is the population-count Fano factor with null 1 and a trip threshold above 1 (placeholder 3, tightened in WP3), plus the single-bin fraction rule, with zero activity handled. Completeness uses the Codex per-neuron input synapse totals as the external denominator and reports "unknown" when they are absent, never an internal estimate.

15. Sensor-to-controller mapping (major). Accepted. The azimuth assignment is labelled an assumption; an encoder-permutation detector reruns the 4.2 protocol under three seeded permutations. The heading update is dimensionally consistent (`sigma_h * sqrt(Δ) * xi`, with `sigma_h` in deg per square-root second). Test 4.1 checks the controller output `omega` as well as the rate contrast, under a frozen stimulus.

16. Behavioural scoring definitions (major). Accepted. Section 3.5 now gives the angular conventions, exact stripe geometry from the wall, preprocessing (missing samples, gap splitting, startup and edge exclusion), walking classification, every metric's definition and denominator, the Brembs import contract (file list, cohort rule, schema mapping, geometry replacement recorded by WP0), and two controls (stripes off, encoder off) in test 4.2. The constant-speed caveat is stated and swept in 4.8. Test 4.0 verifies the metrics on synthetic trajectories.

17. Example cannot isolate the drug effect (blocker). Accepted. The schema gained `arms`; the acceptance experiment is a paired design with identical phases, stimuli, timing, initialisation, and seeds, with a `vehicle` no-op drug; the baseline probe carries the distractor. Criterion 5 is a paired-difference equivalence test with a bootstrap 95 percent interval inside a ±5 percent margin of the vehicle mean, labelled a consequence of the DAT-only model, with the literature cited as context only.

18. Runtime acceptance (major). Accepted. Section 3.10 derives budgets from the complete paired protocol (880 brain seconds), the measured ratio from test 2.5, and four parallel seed workers; at the level 2.5 ceiling of 15 the acceptance budget is 75 minutes. The open-loop fallback has its own status and budget and does not satisfy the closed-loop gate.

19. Storage and live-display contract (major). Accepted. Section 3.8 defines every table's columns and clocks, per-probe drug and settle records, the live NDJSON stream with cadence and content, lifecycle states, atomic writes, manifest-last finalisation, cancellation, worker ownership of directories, WAL-mode index, and valid empty tables. Section 3.9 makes the dashboard tail files rather than receive pushes.

No findings rejected this round.

Section 3.6 gives executable pseudocode: `SeedSequence(S, spawn_key=(seed_id, probe_index, component_id))` with six fixed component ids, `generate_state(1, dtype=np.uint32)` for Brian2, separate `LIVE` and `ANALYSIS` streams, and arms sharing streams by construction. Test 0.8 checks invariance to arm order, worker count, and live on or off.

6. No steady state with DAT-only clearance (major). Accepted. A first-order non-transporter clearance `k_ns` (placeholder 0.05/s, range given) is added, so every state has a finite fixed point given in closed form; K2 sets `alpha_c` against the combined clearance; `reset_fast()` initialises pools at the fixed point of the current composed parameters; the settle criterion is absolute plus relative on DA and rates; zero-rate compartments converge trivially; unsettled runs fail acceptance clause 3. 7. Zero exposure and disabled transporter undefined (major). Accepted. `d_k` is multiplied by the `exposed` mask, so unmapped neurons get identity modulation whatever the receptor map says; uninnervated compartments hold `DA_ref` and are listed. 8. Tests contradict their definitions (major). Accepted. Test 3.7 runs on fixed DA arrays and asserts the removed D1 term and unchanged D2 term separately. 9. Metrics contract requires unsupplied information (major). Accepted. Target selection uses only the displacement direction against the azimuth from the current position to each stripe's wall centre, taking the smaller deviation, so no heading is needed. The displacement window uses the last sample at or before and the first at or after the half-window, within a segment. 10. Calibration staging and work-package gates inconsistent (major). Accepted. Level 0 items owned by WP4 and WP5 are listed in their files. `scripts/calibrate.py` is a stage runner owned by WP0 with stages registered by WP3, WP5, WP9.

11. Proxy as consistency test; undefined shares (major). Accepted. Test 3.6 is a recorded prediction with the proxy caveat. Test 3.8 is a 2×2 factorial (pools × fast synapses) reporting cell means, main-effect contrasts, and the interaction with intervals; no shares.

13. Budget assumes unmeasured speedup (major). Accepted. 14. Schema cannot express the ablation (major). Accepted. Arms may override `dopamine_A` and `dan_fast_synapses` under `comparison: "ablation"`; genotype and protocol belong to arms only; `extra = "forbid"`; numeric domains, tick-aligned durations, unique seed ids and arm labels; matched-arm validation compares resolved phase lists and assay parameters and names the first differing field.
